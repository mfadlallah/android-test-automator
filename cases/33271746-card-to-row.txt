TestRail ID: 33271746
Title: Verify that toggling from card view to row view will display row view

Preconditions:
- The app is installed and the user is already logged in.
- Home is displayed after launch.
- The test transition starts from card view.

Automation setup:
- If Restaurants opens in row view, switch to card view and verify it first.
- Do not count this setup action as the tested card-to-row transition.

Steps:
1. Tap the Restaurants label on Home (or المطاعم in Arabic).
2. Recover safely from any optional foreground sheet.
3. Tap Card Checkbox.
4. Verify Card Checkbox is selected.
5. Tap Row Checkbox.
6. Verify Row Checkbox is selected.
7. Verify that Vendors RecyclerView changed from card view to row view.
8. Scroll down within Vendors RecyclerView.

Expected result:
- Row Checkbox remains selected.
- Vendors RecyclerView items are displayed in row view.
- The list scrolls and reveals moved or additional restaurant content.
