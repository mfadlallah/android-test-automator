TestRail ID: 33271747
Title: Verify that toggling from row view to card view will display card view

Preconditions:
- The app is installed and the user is already logged in.
- Home is displayed after launch.
- The test transition starts from row view.

Automation setup:
- If Restaurants opens in card view, switch to row view and verify it first.
- Do not count this setup action as the tested row-to-card transition.

Steps:
1. Verify app is on Home screen with "What would you like to order?" label visible.
2. Tap the Restaurants label on Home (or المطاعم in Arabic).
3. Recover safely from any optional foreground sheet.
4. Verify that row view (right toggle option) is selected.
5. Tap card view (left toggle option).
6. Verify that restaurant items change from row view to card view.
7. Scroll down within the vertical restaurant list.

Expected result:
- Card view remains selected.
- Restaurant items are displayed in card view.
- The list scrolls and reveals moved or additional restaurant content.
