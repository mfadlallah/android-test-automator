TestRail ID: 33271747
Title: Verify that toggling from row view to card view will display card view

Preconditions:
- The app is installed and the user is already logged in.
- Home is displayed after launch.
- The test transition starts from row view.

Automation setup:
- If Restaurants opens in card view, switch to row view and verify it first.
- Do not count this setup action as the tested row-to-card transition.

Steps:
1. Tap the Restaurants label on Home (or المطاعم in Arabic).
2. Recover safely from any optional foreground sheet.
3. Tap Row Checkbox.
4. Verify Row Checkbox is selected.
5. Tap Card Checkbox.
6. Verify Card Checkbox is selected.
7. Verify that Vendors RecyclerView changed from row view to card view.
8. Scroll down within Vendors RecyclerView.

Expected result:
- Card Checkbox remains selected.
- Vendors RecyclerView items are displayed in card view.
- The list scrolls and reveals moved or additional restaurant content.
