TestRail ID: 33271749
Title: Verify that sorting based on rating updates the list and does not show ad vendors

Preconditions:
- The app is installed and the user is already logged in.
- Home is displayed after launch.

Steps:
1. Tap the Restaurants label on Home (or المطاعم in Arabic).
2. Recover safely from any optional foreground sheet.
3. Verify the Filters pill is visible.
4. Verify that the first restaurant item contains the exact label "Ad" before filtering.
5. Tap the Filters pill to open the filters sheet.
6. Verify that the Filters sheet is now visible.
7. Tap the "Ratings (High To Low)" option under Sorting options to select it.
8. Verify that "Ratings (High To Low)" is now selected.
9. Tap the Apply button to apply the filters.
10. Verify that the Filters pill now contains the indicator label "1".
11. Verify that the Filters sheet is now hidden.
12. Wait until the restaurant items change and finish loading after filtering.
13. Verify that the refreshed restaurant items are now visible.
14. Verify that the first restaurant item does not contain the exact label "Ad" after filtering.
15. Scroll down within the vertical restaurant list.

Expected result:
- The Filters pill contains indicator label "1".
- Refreshed restaurant items are displayed.
- The first restaurant item does not contain the "Ad" label.
- The list scrolls and reveals moved or additional restaurant content.
