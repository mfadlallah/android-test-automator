TestRail ID: T33618525
Title: Verify that clicking on a visible vendor in swimlanes opens its menu

Preconditions:
- The app is installed and the user is already logged in.
- Home is displayed after launch.
- Item 3 means the third identity-tracked item from the observed collection start, not the third simultaneously visible card.

Steps:
1. Tap the Restaurants label on Home (or المطاعم in Arabic).
2. Recover safely from any optional foreground sheet.
3. Scroll down within Vendors RecyclerView until horizontal_scroll is visible (resource-id hint: horizontal_scroll).
4. Verify horizontal_scroll is visible (resource-id hint: horizontal_scroll).
5. Seek item 3 in horizontal_scroll by scrolling left (resource-id hints: horizontal_scroll, vendor_name).
6. Capture tracked item of horizontal_scroll as vendor_title (resource-id hints: horizontal_scroll, vendor_name).
7. Tap tracked item of horizontal_scroll (resource-id hint: horizontal_scroll).
8. Verify vendor_name is visible (resource-id hint: vendor_name).
9. Verify vendor_name contains $vendor_title (resource-id hint: vendor_name).

Expected result:
- A vendor in the horizontal collection is tapped after a scoped horizontal scroll.
- The destination vendor title matches the captured item title.

Notes:
- The tracker first seeks an observed start boundary, then follows overlapping item identities.
- It does not claim the fourth parent position or a backend index. Duplicate titles, lost overlap, or an ungrounded boundary block rather than guess.
