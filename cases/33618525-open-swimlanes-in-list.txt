TestRail ID: T33618525
Title: Verify that clicking on any vendor in swimlanes will direct user to vendor menu screen

Preconditions:
- The app is installed and the user is already logged in.
- Home is displayed after launch.

Steps:
1. Tap the Restaurants label on Home (or المطاعم in Arabic).
2. Recover safely from any optional foreground sheet.
3. Verify that the forth item of Vendors recylerview contains Swimlane horizontal recyclerview .
4. Scroll the Swimlane horizontal recyclerview to third item.
5. Tap on Third item of Swimlane horizontal recyclerview.
6. Verify that Menu screen open and contains same title contained in third item of Swimlane horizontal recyclerview.


Expected result:
- The forth item of vendors recyclerview is visible and contains Swimlane horizontal recyclerview.
- The horizontal recyclerview scrolled left to third item.
- The third item of Swimlane horizontal recyclerview tapped.
- Menu screen is open and contains title equal to title of third item of Swimlane horizontal recyclerview.
