TestRail ID: T33618525
Title: Verify that clicking on any vendor in swimlanes will direct user to vendor menu screen

Preconditions:
- The app is installed and the user is already logged in.
- Home is displayed after launch.

Steps:
1. Tap the Restaurants label on Home (or المطاعم in Arabic).
2. Recover safely from any optional foreground sheet.
3. Scroll down within Vendors RecyclerView until horizontal_scroll is visible (resource-id hint: horizontal_scroll).
4. Verify horizontal_scroll is visible (resource-id hint: horizontal_scroll).
5. Seek item 3 in horizontal_scroll by scrolling left (resource-id hints: horizontal_scroll, vendor_name).
6. Capture vendor_title in tracked item 3 of horizontal_scroll  (resource-id hints: vendor_name).
7. Tap tracked item 3 of horizontal_scroll (resource-id hint: horizontal_scroll).
8. Verify Menu screen is open and contains $vendor_title.

Expected result:
- The Vendors recyclerview scrolled to item number 4.
- Item number 4 in Vendors recyclerview is visible and contains horizontal RecyclerView (resource-id hint: horizontal_scroll).
- The horizontal recyclerview scrolled left to item number 3.
- The item number 3 of horizontal RecyclerView (resource-id hint: horizontal_scroll) tapped successfully.
- Menu screen is open and contains title equal to title of tracked item of horizontal RecyclerView (resource-id hint: horizontal_scroll).
