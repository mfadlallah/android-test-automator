TestRail ID: T33618525
Title: Verify that clicking on any vendor in swimlanes will direct user to vendor menu screen

Preconditions:
- The app is installed and the user is already logged in.
- Home is displayed after launch.

Steps:
1. Tap the Restaurants label on Home (or المطاعم in Arabic).
2. Recover safely from any optional foreground sheet.
3. Verify that the fourth item of Vendors RecyclerView contains horizontal RecyclerView (resource-id hint: horizontal_scroll) .
4. Scroll left within the horizontal RecyclerView (resource-id hint: horizontal_scroll) to reveal its third item.
5. Tap on Third item of horizontal RecyclerView (resource-id hint: horizontal_scroll).
6. Verify that Menu screen open and contains same title contained in third item of horizontal RecyclerView (resource-id hint: horizontal_scroll).


Expected result:
- The fourth item of vendors recyclerview is visible and contains horizontal RecyclerView (resource-id hint: horizontal_scroll).
- The horizontal recyclerview scrolled left to third item.
- The third item of horizontal RecyclerView (resource-id hint: horizontal_scroll) tapped.
- Menu screen is open and contains title equal to title of third item of horizontal RecyclerView (resource-id hint: horizontal_scroll).
