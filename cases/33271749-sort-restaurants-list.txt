TestRail ID: 33271749
Title: Verify that sorting based on rating updates the list and does not show ad vendors

Preconditions:
- The app is installed and the user is already logged in.
- Home is displayed after launch.

Steps:
1. Verify app is on Home screen with "What would you like to order?" label visible.
2. Tap the Restaurants label on Home (or المطاعم in Arabic).
3. Recover safely from any optional foreground sheet.
4. Verify the Filters pill is visible.
5. Verify that the first restaurant item contains the exact label "Ad".
6. Tap the Filters pill.
7. Verify that the Filters sheet is visible.
8. Tap the "Ratings (High To Low)" option under Sorting options.
9. Verify that "Ratings (High To Low)" is selected.
10. Tap the Apply button.
11. Verify that the Filters pill contains the indicator label "1".
12. Verify that the Filters sheet is hidden.
13. Wait until the restaurant items change and finish loading.
14. Verify that the refreshed restaurant items are visible.
15. Verify that the first restaurant item does not contain the exact label "Ad".
16. Scroll down within the vertical restaurant list.

Expected result:
- The Filters pill contains indicator label "1".
- Refreshed restaurant items are displayed.
- The first restaurant item does not contain the "Ad" label.
- The list scrolls and reveals moved or additional restaurant content.
